### AIP
- Definition：Action Improvement Plan — a disciplinary week (Mon-Fri) with automatic payroll consequences including fixed lateness penalties, tripled deductions, and specific day-off rules. Visible on payslips with week dates and explanations.
- Aliases：action improvement plan

### MLA-Ray
- Definition：The sales form format and workflow used for tracking call center sales submissions. Includes specific field structure, approval workflow, and quality review process that the sales log system replicates.
- Aliases：mla ray、mla-ray form

### Working Day
- Definition：Sales submission date accounting rule where sales submitted before 1:00 AM Cairo count on the previous calendar day for payroll and dashboard calculations. Different from submission calendar date.
- Aliases：working day rule、working day calculation

### Quality Ticket
- Definition：Separate review surface for sales quality verification, distinct from the main edit form. Has its own view/edit permissions, assigned verifiers, and workflow for reviewer feedback and client status updates.
- Aliases：quality ticket、quality review

### FP Import
- Definition：Fingerprint attendance import process that uploads biometric device exports to automatically populate attendance records. Uses per-month FP rules and includes audit logging.
- Aliases：fingerprint import、fp attendance import

### Month Lock
- Definition：Payroll finalization control that blocks further edits to attendance and payroll data for completed months. Finance sign-off mechanism preventing changes after month closure.
- Aliases：payroll month lock、month lock

### HS-2 Company
- Definition：Separate company entity within the multi-company architecture. HS-2 remains distinct from the merged HS-1/HS-3 'Hang-Up' company, with separate org structure, permissions, and visibility controls.
- Aliases：hs-2、hs-2 company

### Dual-role TL
- Definition：Team Lead who also holds TL responsibilities across multiple units. Configured via org `tl_employee_id` field, allowing them to see and manage agents in both their home unit and led units.
- Aliases：dual role tl、dual-role team lead
